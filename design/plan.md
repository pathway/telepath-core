# plan

- planning system
- plugs into env

## interface

- given an RL-type model
- explore statespace
- to satisfy a certain objective (reward)
- return a plan: a multi-step trajectory
  - could be deterministic, for deterministic env
  - could be stochastic


## heuristic search like A*, breadth-first, random search, ..

- notion of "goal state"
- notion of "approximate cost / value"
- notion of steps, trees

## organization

- multiple planning engines
